# Festival of Lights: 20 Hanukkah products (2026-11-04)

Hanukkah 2026 starts at sundown on Friday 4 December and runs to 12 December, so
listings need to be live by mid-November to catch gift and craft lead time. The shop
had two Hanukkah products: the Hearth & Snow menorah embroidery (`hearth_menorah`) and
the cement menorah mold (`menorah_mold`). This batch adds twenty, across the three asset
types the workspace can make and sell: machine-embroidery files (`cad stitch`),
polymer-clay cutter sets (`cadkit.cutter`) and cement / Jesmonite molds (`cadkit.relief`).
Every product goes to the shop as a draft for review.

## What the market showed

No hard sales figures exist; Etsy review counts on long-running listings are the best
demand signal (they are not dated, so they show steady sellers, not this year's trend).

- **Embroidery sets with menorah, dreidel and "Let's Get Lit"** lead: a 6-design
  Hanukkah set shows ~28-30k reviews, a "Mini Hanukkah" set ~16k. "Let's Get Lit" with
  a menorah is also a common sweatshirt print. -> `lights_lets_get_lit`, `lights_mini_icons`.
- **"Merry Chrismukkah"** (interfaith households) ~22k reviews. -> `lights_chrismukkah`.
- **Happy Hanukkah with a menorah and Star of David on blue** ~22k; single dreidel
  designs ~16k. -> `lights_dreidel`, `lights_star_olive_wreath`, `lights_festival_of_lights`.
- **Pun and food sayings** sell on sweatshirts: "Oy to the World", "Challah at ya",
  latkes, jelly doughnuts (sufganiyot), gelt. -> `lights_oy_to_the_world`,
  `lights_latke_love`, `lights_sufganiyah`, `lights_gelt`.
- **Quiet / minimal**: "Love and Light", single candles and flames, tonal blue.
  -> `lights_love_and_light`, `lights_blue_candles`.
- **Clay**: dreidel, Star of David and menorah cutters sell as cookie/fondant cutters and
  as polymer-clay earring cutters ($3-24 on Etsy); Judaica earrings are a steady
  polymer-clay niche. -> five cutter sets.
- **Cement / Jesmonite**: modern minimal menorahs and Judaica decor sell on Etsy;
  cement is a natural material for anything holding a flame. We already have the
  menorah mold, so: a Star of David tealight holder, a Star of David coaster and a
  dreidel trinket dish. -> three molds.

Hebrew lettering is common in the market but stays out of this batch: we have no
licensed Hebrew font drawn for embroidery, and a mis-drawn letter is worse than none.
All text is English. No religious text, no licensed characters.
